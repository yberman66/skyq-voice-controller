# Talking to the TV

1. Say **"Hey Siri, Sky"**
2. Wait for it to listen
3. Say one of the words below

## Turning on and off

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| Power | Turns the Sky box on or off |

## Changing channel

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| Channel up | Next channel up |
| Channel down | Next channel down |
| A number, 0 to 9 | Types a channel number, one digit at a time |

## Watching

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| Play / Pause / Stop | Controls what you are watching |
| Rewind / Fast forward | Go back or skip ahead |
| Record | Records the programme |

## Finding your way around

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| Home | Goes to the Sky home screen |
| Apps | Jumps down to the Apps row (Netflix, iPlayer and so on) |
| Netflix | Gets you close to Netflix. Then say Left or Right until it is highlighted, then Select |
| TV guide | Shows what is on |
| Up / Down / Left / Right | Moves the highlight around the screen |
| Select | Chooses whatever is highlighted |
| Back | Goes back one screen |

## Handy tips

- In apps like Netflix, say Left or Right first so something is highlighted, then say Select.
- Apps and Netflix take about 10 seconds. Wait for them to finish and do not say them twice.
- If it does not understand, say it again clearly. Other people talking nearby can confuse it.
- Volume and mute are not available on this controller.
